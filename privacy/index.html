1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NG Training collects, uses, stores, and handles information provided through the NG Training website and related online services.

2. Information We Collect

When users contact NG Training or submit a quote request, we may collect information including:

  • Name and position
  • Organization or company name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • LINE ID or other contact information
  • Event type, date, venue, and participant information
  • Project objectives and approximate budget
  • Quotation and billing information when provided

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, review quote requests, prepare service proposals, communicate with clients, coordinate activities, provide requested services, and maintain appropriate business records.

4. Social Media and Third-Party Services

The NG Training website may display or connect with third-party platforms such as TikTok and other social media services.

Information processed directly by those platforms is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

5. TikTok Integration

NG Training may use TikTok developer services to access authorized public account information and public video information for the purpose of displaying NG Training social media content on this website.

Access to TikTok information is subject to authorization and the permissions provided through TikTok.

6. Data Storage and Security

NG Training takes reasonable measures to protect information collected through the website against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

7. Data Sharing

NG Training does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website, communicate with clients, or provide requested services.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, business administration, legal obligations, and legitimate record keeping.

9. Your Rights

Users may contact NG Training regarding their personal information, including requests to access, correct, update, or request deletion of information where applicable.

10. Changes to This Policy

NG Training may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. The latest version will be published on this page together with the updated date.

11. Contact

Questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted using the contact information provided on the NG Training website.
